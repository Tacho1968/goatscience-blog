Privacy policy

Provisional text pending review by the ULPGC data protection office.

What data is collected

Only what you send when you subscribe by email: your email address, your name, the language you want to receive the newsletter in and, if you choose to share them, your professional profile and your country.

What it is used for

Only to send you the weekly Goat Science summary. Your profile and country, if given, are only used to understand who reads the blog. Your data is not used for any other purpose or shared with third parties.

How the newsletter is sent

The newsletter is sent from a University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria email account, with all recipients in blind copy. No subscriber can see anyone else's address. No external newsletter service is used.

How long it is kept

For as long as you stay subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your details are removed from the mailing list.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe, access, correct or ask for your data to be deleted at any time by writing totacho@ulpgc.es.

This website

This website uses no tracking cookies or analytics tools.

Get the summary every week

One email a week with the new summary, in English or Spanish.

To subscribe, send an email to tacho@ulpgc.es with the subject "Subscribe Goat Science" and these details:

  • Full name
  • Language you want to receive it in: English or Spanish
  • Optional: your profile (farming, veterinary, student, research or other) and your country
Write the subscription email

To unsubscribe at any time, write with the subject "Unsubscribe Goat Science". More information in the privacy policy.
