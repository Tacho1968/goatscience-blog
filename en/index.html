Where does Staphylococcus aureus hide on a dairy goat farm?

S. aureus was found on 27 of 29 Dutch dairy goat farms, in goats, on workers' hands and in the environment. Airborne dust was where it was detected most often, and strains circulated mainly within each farm.

Adetunji MFM, Pals MAM, Libera K, Kampfraath AA, Koop G, Vrieling M, Smit LAM (2026). A cross-sectional study of Staphylococcus aureus reservoirs in dairy goats, their environment and farm workers. One Health.
